Compose a request for the swarm

Pick what to ask for, fill in the fields and check the problems on the right. Quote is free: the control plane rebuilds the request and tells you whether it is valid. You only pay with Pay, signing from your wallet (0.5 IMD).

Or ask your own agent Claude, Codex or any assistant writes the JSON for you
  1. Copy the prompt below and paste it into your agent.
  2. Replace the last line with what you want the swarm to do.
  3. Paste the JSON it answers with into JSON body → Edit and press Apply to form. The checks on the right tell you what to fix.
